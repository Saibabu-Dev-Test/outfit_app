About Outfit Vote

Android app · package com.saibabu.outfit

Outfit Vote is an Android app where you post photos of your outfits and your friends vote on them with a simple A–F grade, so you can decide what to wear. You can also keep a digital wardrobe of your clothes and accessories.

What you can do

  • Post outfits and get A–F votes and comments from friends, family and circles.
  • Manage your wardrobe: save clothes, shoes and accessories with photos.
  • Earn coins (optional): complete surveys, offers and tasks from third-party partners on the Earn tab.

How coins and withdrawals work

  • 100 coins = ₹1.
  • For surveys and offers from partners, you receive 65% of what the partner pays us for the task you complete. What each task pays is set by the partner and is never guaranteed.
  • Coins shown in partner walls are before Outfit's share; you receive 65%.
  • After you finish a task and leave a partner wall, Outfit may show one ad. These ads help run the app and do not give coins.
  • New earnings are held for 3–7 days while the partner verifies the completion. A partner can reverse a completion it finds invalid, and coins from reversed or fraudulent activity are removed.
  • You can withdraw to your UPI ID once you have at least ₹50. Every withdrawal is reviewed and approved by an admin before it is paid. We never charge a fee and never ask for your UPI PIN or bank password.
