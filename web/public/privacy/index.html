Privacy Policy

Outfit Vote (Android app, package com.saibabu.outfit) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Outfit Vote lets you share outfit photos, get votes and comments from friends and circles, keep a wardrobe, read astrology and festival information, and earn coins through optional tasks. This page explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. The app is run by Sai Babu, Mumbai, India.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your Google account email and name when you sign in with Google (Firebase Authentication), and the mobile number you choose from your SIM. We allow one account per Gmail and per mobile number.
  • Profile and content: your display name, profile photo, outfit photos, wardrobe items and tags, votes, comments, chat messages, circles, follows and likes.
  • Astrology details (optional): date, time and place of birth you enter for astrology features.
  • Approximate location (optional): only if you allow it, used for nearby/regional features. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Earn feature (optional): your coin balance and earning history, completed tasks, and the UPI ID you enter to withdraw. A one-way hash of your UPI ID and of your device's Android ID is stored to enforce one account per UPI ID and per phone; the raw Android ID never leaves your phone.
  • Technical data: a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and basic app/device information needed to run the service.

The fingerprint app lock uses Android's own biometric system; your fingerprint never leaves your phone and is never seen by us. Photo background removal, face blur, wardrobe auto-tags and translation run on your phone (Google ML Kit).

2. Where your data is stored

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging) stores your account, profile, posts, votes, comments, chats, coins and withdrawal requests.
  • Photos are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and, when that is full, in Cloudinary (cloudinary.com). Photos you post are visible to the people you share them with.
  • If you use the AI stylist, the photo/text you send for a suggestion is processed by Google Gemini to create the answer.

3. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, including rewarded ads in the Earn tab. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's ads policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads.

4. Surveys and offerwall partners

The optional Earn tab opens surveys and offers from CPX Research and Offerwall.GG. When you open them we pass an anonymous user ID so they can credit your coins. Any answers or details you give inside a survey or offer go directly to that partner under their own privacy policy: CPX Research, Offerwall.GG.

5. UPI withdrawals

To withdraw coins as money you enter your UPI ID. We use it only to send your payment after an admin approves the request, and to stop the same UPI ID being used by more than one account. We never ask for your UPI PIN, bank password or card details, and we never charge you any fee.

6. How we use and share data

  • To run the app: sign-in, showing your posts to the people you choose, votes, chats, notifications, coins and payouts.
  • To prevent fraud and duplicate accounts in the Earn feature.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers named above, or when required by Indian law.

7. Data deletion and your rights

You can delete your own posts, comments and photos in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and all its data (profile, photos in Firebase and Cloudinary, votes, chats, coins and UPI details), email saibabu140596@gmail.com from your account's Gmail with the subject "Outfit Vote data deletion". We delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records). You can also ask for a copy or correction of your data the same way.

8. Children

Outfit Vote is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security and changes

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by Firebase security rules. We will update this page when the app changes and show the new date at the top.
