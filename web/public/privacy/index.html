Privacy Policy

Outfit Vote (Android app, package com.saibabu.outfit) · Effective: 16 November 2026

Outfit Vote lets you share outfit photos, get A–F votes and comments from friends and circles, keep a wardrobe, and earn coins through optional surveys, offers and tasks. This page explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. The app is run by Sai Babu, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name from Google / Firebase sign-in, and the mobile number you choose from your SIM. We allow one account per email and per mobile number.
  • Profile: display name, profile photo, gender and date of birth you enter when registering (date, time and place of birth only if you use the optional astrology features).
  • Your content: outfit photos you upload, wardrobe items and tags, votes, comments, chat messages, circles, follows and likes.
  • Approximate location (optional): only if you allow it, used to show weather for your area and events. We do not collect precise GPS tracking.
  • Earn feature (optional): your coin balance and earning history, completed tasks, and the UPI ID you enter to withdraw. A one-way hash of your UPI ID and of your device's Android ID is stored to enforce one account per UPI ID and per phone.
  • Device information: device model, Android version, app version, a push-notification token, and advertising ID (used by ads, see section 3).

The fingerprint app lock uses Android's own biometric system; your fingerprint never leaves your phone. Photo background removal, face blur, wardrobe auto-tags and translation run on your phone (Google ML Kit).

2. Services we use to run the app

  • Google Firebase: Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (database), Cloud Storage (photos) and Cloud Messaging (notifications).
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1: our server code and a backup database for app data, coins and partner reward callbacks.
  • Cloudinary: extra photo storage when Firebase storage is full.
  • Google Gemini: if you use the AI stylist, the photo/text you send is processed to create the suggestion.

3. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, including rewarded ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show, measure and personalise ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires consent for personalised ads, you are asked before they are shown. You can opt out of personalised ads or delete your advertising ID any time in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads; you will then see non-personalised ads.

4. Surveys and offerwall partners

The optional Earn tab opens surveys and offers from BitLabs, CPX Research and Offerwall.GG. When you open them we pass your app user ID so the partner can tell us which tasks you completed and we can credit your coins. Answers or details you give inside a survey or offer go directly to that partner under their own privacy policy: BitLabs, CPX Research, Offerwall.GG.

5. How we use and share data

  • To run the app: sign-in, showing your posts to the people you choose, votes, chats, weather, notifications, coins and payouts.
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate accounts and invalid survey or offer completions.
  • We use your UPI ID only to pay approved withdrawals. We never ask for your UPI PIN, bank password or card details.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers and partners named above, or when required by Indian law.

6. Data deletion and your rights

You can delete your own posts, comments and photos in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and its data, or to ask for a copy or correction of your data, email 2saibabug@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, subject “Outfit Vote data deletion”. We delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records).

7. Children

Outfit Vote is not for children under 13, and they may not create an account. We do not knowingly collect their data; if you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security and changes

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by Firebase security rules. We will update this page when the app changes and show the new date at the top.
